Stories we want to see

To communicate the risks of advanced AI through film, you want to move away from Terminator-style robots and toward structural, systemic, and biological failures. In the d/acc (defensive acceleration) framework, these films work as red-teaming scenarios — showing what we need to build defenses against.

Films we support should still end with a credible defensive solution and, where possible, a hopeful note: d/acc — decentralized tools, transparency, and cooperation — helping humanity steer these risks.

Category I

Biological & health threats — bio-weaponization

Focus: how AI lowers the barrier to creating or spreading pathogens.

  • The Optimization Loop

    A bio-hacker uses a local AI to "optimize" a probiotic. It succeeds — but the new bacteria is so efficient it outcompetes the user's entire gut microbiome, causing a slow, unstoppable wasting disease.

Cyber & economic threats

Focus: the collapse of trust, financial stability, and digital identity.

  • Flash Crash 2.0

  • The Deepfake Ledger

    A film shot entirely through Zoom screens where a CEO is convinced by his "board" to liquidate the company — only to realize every person on the call was a real-time AI avatar.

  • The Ransomware Colony

  • Epistemic Decay
